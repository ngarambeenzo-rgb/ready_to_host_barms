📱 How to install on Android
Your bar's link
chezpatrick.barms.rw
1
Open Chrome browser
Make sure you're using Google Chrome — not another browser
2
Type your bar's link
Type chezpatrick.barms.rw in the address bar and press Enter
3
Tap "Install App"
A banner will appear at the bottom. Tap "Install" or look for the install icon in the address bar
4
Open from home screen
The BarMS Pro icon now appears on your home screen. Tap it to open!

⚠️ No internet? The app still works! You can view data and record sales. Everything syncs automatically when internet returns.

Login with your username and password given by your manager
Change your password after first login!

🍎 How to install on iPhone / iPad
Your bar's link
chezpatrick.barms.rw
1
Open Safari browser
Must use Safari on iPhone/iPad — Chrome does not support install on iOS
2
Type your bar's link
Type chezpatrick.barms.rw in the address bar and press Go
3
Tap the Share button
Tap the Share icon ↑ at the bottom of Safari (square with arrow pointing up)
4
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the share menu and tap "Add to Home Screen" then tap "Add"
5
Open from home screen
The BarMS Pro icon appears. Tap it — app opens fullscreen!

⚠️ No internet? The app still works! You can view data and record sales. Everything syncs automatically when internet returns.

Login with your username and password given by your manager
Change your password after first login!
